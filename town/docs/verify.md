# What to check of the town

The town's points, as a user checks them in Sonar with the extension installed. They were in Sonar's map of features (`.agents/skills/verify/features.md` of Sonar's repository) while the town was part of Sonar. The French words in quotes are the interface's, as a French user reads them. The tests named are this folder's (`src/`, `tests/page/`), unless they are said to be Sonar's.

## The town

- With the extension "Ville" installed and switched on (Settings › Extensions; a new Sonar does not have it), on the map only, and on a computer only, the icon "Visualisation" (a cube) turns the page into an isometric town, sharp, without pixel art: the icon turns while the town is prepared, then goes down to the bottom right corner; each card dissolves into small bricks that build its subject's building and its inhabitant, and the background into dust that becomes the ground, without a white flash and without a jolt. The whole page (cards, zones, arrows, dotted background) comes apart along one front from left to right, its edge lit in the accent colour, without a fade. The subjects' names are whole and do not overlap. With "reduce motion", the town appears built at once. A subject = a block, an agent = an inhabitant. The town goes under the top bar, without a band of the map above it. Another tab hides it; the map shows it again as it was. The same icon brings the whole page back: each card comes back with its bricks, never before, even if an agent worked or a session opened while the town showed (the map is updated once the page is whole). A zone of the map comes apart and back with the background, and a subject seen from afar (its summary, its cards hidden) leaves and comes back whole: nothing of the map shows whole behind the town, at any zoom level, even if the map's plan changed while the town showed. While the town comes or goes back, a drag or the wheel neither turns nor zooms it.
- As the town comes, the whole page leaves along one edge, in two dissolves at the same moment that the eye tells apart: the background and the zones leave as fine pale dust that flies low; each card breaks, as the edge passes, into small sharp bricks in the accent colour (light top, two dark sides), which rise in a sheaf above the card and fall on its subject's building. No card stays whole or keeps its shape, and none leaves after the page around it; a text leaves just after its background. Between the page's edge and the town, the town shows first as a wireframe: a mesh of thin lines, just before its cubes. No glass: no crack, no piece that lifts, no reflection. Nothing leaves at once and nothing flickers, on a dense screen too. A subject's block never lands before the cubes of its cards, wherever its cards are on the page: until its cards have left, its block stays a wireframe; its ground rises in a wave, column by column, where its points land (no point stays in the air on the wireframe), then its building rises storey by storey with its bricks. Going back to the map plays it all backwards, and the page comes back whole: a building gives back its bricks before its cards are whole.
- A touch device (phone, tablet) has no town icon. The town is drawn whole or not offered: a machine whose graphics card cannot hold it is told so once, and the icon comes back the next day.
- The town's size follows the number of agents, from 10 to 10 000: each agent shows. From afar, the agents of a place are a round mark, a cloud of points that says their number; near, each one is an inhabitant of 15 px at least; its name shows above its head when there is room (two names at most per zone of the screen). With 10 000 agents, the town opens in less than 60 s. A subject's overflow stands on a paved yard of its size (4 agents at least), or on esplanades of a whole block at the town's front edge.
- The town's camera turns, tilts and zooms on three levels; a compass straightens it. The subjects' signs follow their roof without shaking. By night (dark theme), the lamps light up and a light line underlines the foot of the base.
- Each project has a town of a style of its own (buildings, colours, monument), among six. Without a choice, the extension draws once the style the other projects have the least, and keeps it: a project's town does not change its look when another project comes or goes.
- Settings → project → General, section "Ville", on a computer only (where the town's icon is offered): a picture per style, by day or by night as the theme, and first "Automatique", the draw. A click saves at once, without "Enregistrer"; a save refused puts the choice before back and says why. A town open in another style closes: its next opening builds it in the style chosen. The test bench has no icon: set `sonar:town-draw` as [`tests/page/support/town-draw.ts`](../tests/page/support/town-draw.ts) does.
- In the town, a session's subagents become robots, those of a session of a linked computer too; an agent at rest and its robots stand in the clear of the square, never hidden by a tree or a facade.
- The open town follows its agents without being opened again: a session that opens has an inhabitant born at its place, in sparks from the door of its building; a session closed leaves it; an agent that changes state leaves its former place and is born at the new one, in its new colour, with its crate, its bubble or its mark; a subagent that starts has a robot born from its agent's chest, a subagent done turns grey. The neighbours glide to their new place, the subject's sign and the names follow. Twelve sessions opened together come in a wave, not at once. With "reduce motion", on another tab or a hidden window, the change is there at once. The camera never moves by itself. Test: `tests/page/world-life.page.ts`; the effect is watched in Edge, on a real graphics card.
- In the town, a subject with an open pull request carries scaffolding around its building's storey to come, born in sparks from the door, and its sign carries a bar per pull request, filled as its checks end (5 bars at most, then "+N"). A check fails: the bar and the planks turn red, after a short shake. The pull request merged or closed: the scaffolding goes back to the door and the bar goes. A town opened anew shows the same picture as a town that followed live. With "reduce motion", the change is there at once. Test: `tests/page/world-pulls.page.ts` (the bench has no pull request: the test tells them to the page as the server tells them).
- In the town, the height of a subject's building says its merged work: a storey, then one more each time its merges double (after 1, 3, 7, 15… merges; 9 storeys at most). Between two steps, each merge lays its share of the storey to come, under the roof (4, 5 and 6 merges: a quarter, half, three quarters of the fourth storey): the share laid shows as the rest of the building, and what is left to lay is under a green building site's net, finely meshed, which gets smaller at each merge; no hole nor point in the accent colour, from afar as near, in the light theme as the dark. Sonar's server counts the merges of each session and keeps them without a time limit (`~/.sonar/merged.json`): a merged pull request, or the branch of a session without a pull request arrived in its base branch; the subject's closed sessions count, and a subject renamed keeps its count. At the first start, the count starts from the merges announced still known (two weeks). In a town of 200 agents and more, all the subjects' buildings keep seven storeys. Tests: Sonar's `apps/server/src/fleet/merged.test.ts`, `tests/page/world-merges.page.ts` (the bench has no merge: the test tells the count to the page as the server tells it).
- In the open town, a merge counted for a subject is laid on its building: a crane is born beside it, a truck comes along the street with a pallet in the wall's colour, the crane lifts the pallet, the pallet pours its bricks in a sheaf and the share of the storey this merge lays appears under the site's net, which gets smaller as much; the roof and the sign rise with a storey begun, the net under the roof. Then the truck and the crane leave. Scaffolding surrounds the storey to come while it is laid, as for an open pull request, and the merged pull request's bar stays on the sign, full, until the laying ends. One truck at a time; over 3 merges waiting, the oldest are laid at once. A pull request closed without a merge, or a merge on a building of 9 storeys, brings no truck. The town that followed the merge shows the same picture as a town opened after. With "reduce motion", the storey is there at once. In a town of 200 agents and more, nothing comes. Tests: `src/sites.test.ts`, `src/landing.test.ts`, `tests/page/world-merge-build.page.ts`.
- In the open town, a session that opens on a subject without a building builds its building under the user's eyes, on the block the town keeps for it: what stood there leaves as bricks, scaffolding is born on the ground, the building rises from the bottom up with it, then the scaffolding leaves; the subject's sign shows once the building is built, then its inhabitant is born at the door. The ground stays. The camera does not move by itself. One block per opening: a second new subject shows at the next opening. A session not filed yet does not take the block: it is for the subject it receives. At the next opening, the building may change its place in front of it, its roof or its facade. With "reduce motion", or for what changed while the town was not watched, the building and its inhabitant are there at once. Tests: `src/plot.test.ts`, `tests/page/world-new-topic.page.ts`.
- In the town, an inhabitant at work carries its agent's tool in place of its crate, without leaving its round: a book when the agent reads or searches (files or the web), a hammer when it writes, a terminal when it runs a command; its arms follow (book held, hammer raised, hands that type). Any other tool keeps the crate. When the agent changes tools, the object before shrinks and the next grows in 0.35 s, without a birth; with "reduce motion", or for what changed while the town was not watched, the object is there at once. A town opened anew shows the same object as a town that followed. Nothing is made up: the tool is the last one the server says of the session, and its family comes from the agent's catalogue. Tests: `src/live.test.ts`, `src/sources.test.ts`, `tests/page/world-tools.page.ts`.
- In the town, from 4 agents waiting for the user on a subject's square, their bubbles fly in 0.45 s into one large bubble above the group, which carries their number written in the middle of its ball; at 3 or fewer, each one takes its bubble back above its head. The large bubble comes overshooting its size then settles, and the small ones jump on the way. The number follows the fleet: it rises when one more agent waits, it falls when the user answers; at each change, the ball beats once and the digits roll in 0.3 s. With "reduce motion", or for what changed while the town was not watched, everything is in place at once. From afar, the subject's round mark already counts those who wait: the number shows only when the inhabitants can be read, never with the mark. A town opened anew shows the same bubble as a town that followed. The number stays in the middle of the ball whatever the camera's heading, and fades when a building hides the bubble. A small bubble's "!" is in the middle of its ball, and the bubbles face the camera when it turns. Tests: `src/heaps.test.ts`, `src/bubbles.test.ts`, `tests/page/world-heaps.page.ts`.
- In the town, the mouse resting 0.35 s on an inhabitant shows a card: its session's name, its state and since when, what it asks or said last, its pull requests. A click on the inhabitant pins its notebook: the session's 3 last facts, the latest on top, and the button "Ouvrir la session", which opens the session. The card comes out of the inhabitant, glides from one inhabitant to the next, and unfolds line by line at the click; with "reduce motion", it is in place at once. Escape, the cross or a click elsewhere closes the notebook; it closes too when its inhabitant leaves the screen or its session closes; a drag of the town pins nothing. When the session changes while the notebook is pinned, its lines are updated without the button or the cross flickering. In a crowd, the card is the inhabitant's nearest the pointer. Tests: `src/note-under.test.ts`, `tests/page/world-notebook.page.ts`.
- In the open town, a short sound for each true event seen on the screen, silence otherwise, without background sound: bricks landing (a new block or a merge's storey), a merge entered in the building, a storey or a building done, a pull request's scaffolding laid, its tests failing, an inhabitant coming or leaving. They are Sonar's sounds played lower, tuned in C, placed left or right by where they are, three times softer seen from afar. Nothing for a place off the screen, for what the town lays at once (opening, town not watched, "reduce motion"), when the sounds are cut, in a tab that does not play the sounds, nor before the first gesture on the page. A town back on the screen (tab hidden, another view shown) does not play what it missed, and several events of the same kind at the same moment (forty sessions opened together) make one sound. Nothing plays once the town is closed. Settings → Sounds, folded group "La ville" after "Tes actions", on a computer only (where the town is offered): 4 lines, each with "Écouter ce son" and a switch, set on this device; the scaffolding of a new building or of a merge follows the bricks' line, and cutting "Une PR en test" cuts only the pull requests. Tests: `src/town-sound.test.ts`, `tests/page/world-sounds.page.ts`, Sonar's `tests/page/sounds.page.ts`.
- In the town, an inhabitant passing behind a wall, a roof or a kiosk stays visible as a light silhouette, with a line in its state's colour, never as a solid fill of that colour. To check in Edge, on a real graphics card (WebGPU): the tests' browser draws with WebGL 2, where the fault does not exist.
- The town's messages at the top of the screen are signed "Ville", as any extension's are.

## Without the town

- Without the extension "Ville", or with it switched off, the Map has no town icon and a project's general settings have no block "Ville"; the page downloads none of the town's code.
- A Sonar where a project kept a town style before the town was an extension: once the extension is installed and switched on, each project's town has that style again, chosen or drawn (`tests/page/town-style-left.page.ts`).
