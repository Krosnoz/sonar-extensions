# The town

Where each subject's code lives, folder by folder: [`README.md`](../README.md#where-things-are).

The "Visualisation" icon, in the map's tools, turns the map itself into an isometric town, drawn sharp at the screen's resolution, and back ([`wireTown`](../src/page/sonar/toggle.ts#wireTown)).
While the town shows, the icon waits in the bottom right corner ([`corner`](../src/page/sonar/toggle.ts#corner)).
The town shows only what the map shows: a block and its building per subject, an inhabitant per agent, a small robot per subagent at work.
It is read only: a sign zooms in on its subject, and nothing acts on an agent but an inhabitant's notebook, which opens its session.
The town is an extension, `town`, which Sonar runs once it is installed and switched on. Sonar starts its page entry ([`start`](../src/page/start.ts#start)) with the extension's API (`PageApi` of `@sonar/extension`): the town takes Sonar's parts from that API alone ([`sonar`](../src/page/sonar/sonar.ts#sonar)), and a Sonar that lacks one of them does not start it. Its section (`ui.section`, place `sections`) gives the block "Ville" of a project's general screen. Switched off, neither shows, and the page downloads none of the town's code.

## Loading

The town and three.js load as the pointer or the keyboard reaches the town's icon, by `import()`: Bun puts them in a chunk of their own, and a page that never reaches the icon never downloads them ([`made`](../src/page/sonar/toggle.ts#made)).
three.js draws with WebGPU, else WebGL 2; a browser that gives neither says so in a toast.
Each project's town has a style (its houses, its palette, its monument: [`STYLES`](../src/shared/styles.ts#STYLES)), kept by the extension's server part ([`start`](../src/server.ts#start), [`readKept`](../src/server/style-store.ts#readKept)): the one the user chose, else a draw, the style the fewest other projects' towns show ([`leastTaken`](../src/shared/world.ts#leastTaken)). The page reads it while the town's code loads, and asks for the draw the first time ([`townStyle`](../src/page/style/town-style.ts#townStyle)); the server part draws on that request alone, never on a reading. While the server cannot be read, the town takes a style from the project's name ([`styleFor`](../src/shared/styles.ts#styleFor)). The user chooses it in the project's general screen, on a machine that is offered the town only: a picture per style, served among the extension's files (`/ext/town/towns/<name>`), saved at the click ([`townStylePane`](../src/page/style/town-style-pane.ts#townStylePane)). A town built in another style ends, and its next opening builds the style chosen ([`restyled`](../src/page/sonar/toggle.ts#restyled)).
The town is built in steps that give the browser its hand back ([`stepped`](../src/page/render/steps.ts#stepped)): built in one go, it froze the page 2 to 3 s.
Its drawing programs are prepared before anything moves, while the icon turns ([`prepared`](../src/page/camera/view.ts#prepared), [`pipeline`](../src/page/render/pipeline.ts#pipeline)): prepared by the first frame, they froze the page for seconds. The card starts on them as soon as the town is built; those `compileAsync` leaves out (the sun's shadow, the glow) are made aside in a hidden first frame, before the probe ([`warmed`](../src/page/render/warm.ts#warmed)).
An opening that takes longer than a minute, from the click to the town built, stops at the next step of its build or at once while the town arrives: the page comes back whole, and the machine is told it does not hold the town, as by the probe ([`OPENING_LIMIT`](../src/page/sonar/toggle.ts#OPENING_LIMIT)).
While the town is prepared or leaves, its icon turns and says it is busy: a click then waits for the end ([`preparing`](../src/page/sonar/toggle.ts#preparing)).
From the reading of its cards to the page whole again, the map draws no card again: the town is cut out of those cards and flies back into them, and a card drawn again or added under it has no brick to come back from. The town covers the Map (`PageMap`'s `cover`): the Map keeps the last sessions it was given and draws them once the town uncovers it ([`whole`](../src/page/sonar/toggle.ts#whole)). What lays it out without new sessions waits too, as under a drag: its plan read again, another look, the fonts (Sonar's Map does so). While the map waits, it tells the town the sessions it would draw, and the town shows them ([The town follows its agents](#the-town-follows-its-agents)).
While the town comes or goes, it takes no gesture, and still takes the pointer so the map under it stays still ([`gestures`](../src/page/camera/gestures.ts#gestures), the `moving` class of [`open`](../src/page/sonar/toggle.ts#open)).
On the island's ground, the wheel keeps the point under the pointer under it, its front edge included; off it, and zooming out, the camera's bound holds ([`zoomAt`](../src/page/camera/camera-follow.ts#zoomAt), [`bound`](../src/page/camera/bounds.ts#bound)).
Another tab hides the town without ending it, and stops drawing it; the Map shows it again as it was ([`follow`](../src/page/sonar/toggle.ts#follow)).
Another project, or the projects' home, ends it at once, without its way back ([`leave`](../src/page/sonar/toggle.ts#leave)).

## From the page to the town

1. The map is read as it shows, as the Map gives it (`PageMap`'s `cards`): its cards by island ([`readMap`](../src/page/sonar/sources.ts#readMap)).
2. Each card and zone is cut into cells of the colours the page shows there ([`sheetsOf`](../src/page/arrival/sheets.ts#sheetsOf), [`cut`](../src/page/arrival/cells.ts#cut)). Only what the page really shows is cut or gives a colour: a card hidden by its opacity or its visibility, as in an island seen from far, is not cut on its own, and its island leaves whole ([`shows`](../src/page/arrival/cells.ts#shows)).
3. The town is mounted over the page, transparent where it is not built yet ([`mount`](../src/page/render/kit.ts#mount)); a front builds it at a constant speed, in small cubes, a building in its own colours without a glow. Just ahead of the front, the town shows as a frame: the lines of its cubes' lattice, one in four, light up in the user's accent a moment before the cubes are laid there, behind the page's edge ([`frameOf`](../src/page/render/part-material.ts#frameOf)). The frame is light alone and casts no shadow; the people have none.
4. The page leaves along one edge, which follows the front that builds the town, from left to right, in tongues ([`edgeOf`](../src/page/arrival/edge.ts#edgeOf)). Its ground, its zones and its cards leave as the edge reaches them, at the same instant: a card kept whole while the page round it left, or that left a moment after it, read as a card that does not dissolve. A text leaves 0.12 s after the ground round it ([`timed`](../src/page/arrival/flights.ts#timed)).
   The page itself is never touched, copied or moved: behind the edge, a cover of the colour behind the page hides it, one canvas of a pixel for a case of a few pixels of the screen, painted case by case as the edge reaches each; each frame changes only the cases the edge reached ([`covering`](../src/page/arrival/cover.ts#covering)). A mask image on the page's things, or a shape cut in the map, made the browser draw the page again at each change, and the page flickered.
5. The page leaves in two dissolves the eye tells apart ([`lookOf`](../src/page/arrival/flights.ts#lookOf)). The ground's cells become dust: points of one pixel of the drawing, pale, with a short trace, which fly low to a tile of their block or fall under the town ([`dotsOf`](../src/page/arrival/dots.ts#dotsOf)). A card, and a zone seen from far, are cut three times coarser ([`sheetsOf`](../src/page/arrival/sheets.ts#sheetsOf)), and their cells become bricks: small cubes in the user's accent, sharp and without a trace, their top paler and two sides darker, which leave in a spray high over their card and come down onto a brick of their subject's building or a part of its inhabitant ([`flights`](../src/page/arrival/flights.ts#flights), [`dust`](../src/page/arrival/dust.ts#dust), [`bricks`](../src/page/arrival/places.ts#bricks)). Dust and bricks leave at the same instant, and each lands when its place is laid.
   A subject's block is laid by the cubes of its cards, never before them. The town's plan does not follow where the cards stand on the page: a card right of its block leaves after the front has passed the block. Each block holds how much further the front must go before it is laid ([`delays`](../src/page/arrival/delays.ts#delays)), set at each opening from its cubes' flights, a cube flying 0.6 s at least ([`waits`](../src/page/arrival/brick-waits.ts#waits), [`SHORTEST`](../src/page/arrival/brick-waits.ts#SHORTEST)). The whole block waits, its land, its street and its trees. Its land is laid column by column, where each of its cubes lies along the front, as the point of that column lands ([`tiles`](../src/page/arrival/places.ts#tiles)): laid as a whole, from its one instance at the block's centre, it left the points of its near half lying in the air. Then its building rises, from its ground floor up, each storey as its bricks land ([`laid`](../src/page/arrival/brick-waits.ts#laid)). A block whose cards left early waits for nothing, and a wait never outlasts the front. The parts of a subject's block, the figures, the cars and the night's lines of light read the wait of the block they stand on ([`built`](../src/page/render/part-material.ts#built), [`Waits`](../src/page/arrival/delays.ts#Waits)); the rest of the town reads none.
   The construction's time drives every flight and the cover: the way back runs it backwards. It advances by each frame's time, a twentieth of a second at most, so a late frame slows it and never skips a part ([`paced`](../src/page/arrival/morph.ts#paced)).
6. Over the page, in the user's accent, a thin edge sparks on the cells about to leave ([`rim`](../src/page/arrival/rim.ts#rim)).
## A figure born or gone

The figure of an agent, or of one of its subagents, is born and goes in layers, on the town's own clock, once the town is there.

- Each instance of the people's parts has a mark ([`Part`](../src/page/render/parts.ts#Part), `mark`): 0, it is there; over 0, it is born at that second of the town; under 0, it goes from that second.
- The part's material cuts the figure in plates of uneven shapes, of its own model, and goes through them from its top down: the seams between the plates light up in the accent the user chose, a plate's fill goes, the lattice under it stays lit and burns away ([`layered`](../src/page/people/layers.ts#layered), [`orderOf`](../src/page/people/layers.ts#orderOf)). A line is a pixel of the drawing wide at least. What gives the figure's light (a robot's eyes and antenna) shines, then goes with the last plates. A birth is the going run backwards. The silhouettes and the shadow follow the same mask.
- The figure's matter flies back to what it belongs to, and comes from there at a birth: a robot's to its agent's chest, an inhabitant's to its building's door ([`grainsOf`](../src/page/people/layer-grains.ts#grainsOf)). The grains are few sparks, thin streaks drawn along their way, white at the head and the accent along the tail, with some chips in the figure's colours; they meet on one point, where a flare shines while they land ([`sparks`](../src/page/people/layer-sparks.ts#sparks)). They fly on the town's seconds; a flight follows what it leaves and what it lands on, which may walk ([`flying`](../src/page/people/layer-grains.ts#flying)).
- The seams, the sparks and the flare are brighter than white, so the glow pass spreads their light ([`pipeline`](../src/page/render/pipeline.ts#pipeline)). By night it is the night's glow. By day the glow is drawn only while a figure is born or goes, with a threshold nothing the sun lights reaches: the rest of the town does not change.
- An agent's name goes as its figure starts to go, and comes back once the figure is whole ([`Figure`](../src/page/render/kit.ts#Figure), `away`).
- The town plays it for a figure it has, found by the agent's handle and the subagent's rank ([`played`](../src/page/people/births.ts#played), [`Mounted`](../src/page/render/kit.ts#Mounted), `play`). The town plays it itself when an agent changes (below); the event `town-life` on the town's host plays it on demand, for a check ([`openWorld`](../src/page/camera/view.ts#openWorld)).

## The town follows its agents

While the town is open, it shows the fleet as it is, not as it was at its opening. Nothing is made up: each movement comes from a change of a session, the camera never moves by itself, and the town stays read only.

- The Map, covered by the town, tells it the sessions it would draw at each change (`PageMap`'s `cover`, its `watch`). The page turns them into who is wanted: a session's subject, its state, its name, its subagents ([`wantedOf`](../src/page/sonar/sources.ts#wantedOf)), with the states the cut of the map reads ([`readMap`](../src/page/sonar/sources.ts#readMap)).
- The town compares who is wanted with who it shows, figure by figure, and plays what differs ([`living`](../src/page/people/live.ts#living), [`step`](../src/page/people/live-steps.ts#step)). A session that opens: an inhabitant is born at its place, from its building's door. One that closes: it goes back to the door, and its place is free again. A state that changes: the inhabitant goes from its old place while it is born at the new one, in its new colour, with what it carries; a second body, kept hidden, plays the going ([`ghostOf`](../src/page/people/bodies.ts#ghostOf)). A subject that changes: it goes from one subject's place and is born on the other's. A subagent that starts: a robot is born from its agent's chest; one that ends turns grey, without an effect; one no longer listed goes back to the chest.
- Who only changes place, because a neighbour came or went, glides there in 0.4 s ([`strides`](../src/page/people/crowd-lay.ts#strides)). After each change, a place stands as if the town had opened on its agents: the same rule lays both ([`lay`](../src/page/people/crowd-lay.ts#lay), [`crowdOf`](../src/page/people/crowd.ts#crowdOf)).
- A part's instances are counted when the town is built, and a place's ones follow each other in its lot: none can be added later. Each place is built with spare figures, hidden, that a new session takes and a closed one gives back: 12 inhabitants and 12 robots ([`SPARE`](../src/page/people/bodies.ts#SPARE), [`people`](../src/page/people/crowd-lay.ts#people)). A figure is written again in place, never put ([`Part`](../src/page/render/parts.ts#Part), `set`). A session past a place's spares shows at the town's next opening; one of a subject the town has not built, on the block the town holds for it (see "A subject that comes").
- Figures of one place are born or go 0.18 s apart: twelve sessions opened together come as a wave, not as a flash. In the whole town, two figures at most start in one frame: a figure's grains take some 10 ms to make, and many agents that change in one message would make a late frame. Past 40 figures born or going at once in the whole town, and for a place the camera does not show, a change is there at once, without layers nor grains.
- What one at work holds says what its agent does now: a book while it reads or searches, a hammer while it writes, a console while it runs a command, its crate with any other tool ([`tools`](../src/page/people/tools.ts#tools)). The tool is the last one the Map says of its session (`MapSession`'s `tool`), and what it does is its kind as its agent says it ("read", "edit", "shell"): the town knows no tool by its name ([`readMap`](../src/page/sonar/sources.ts#readMap)). The figure's arms say it too: its state, as its shader reads it, carries what it holds ([`codeOf`](../src/page/people/tools.ts#codeOf)). An agent that takes another tool, still at work, is not born again: what it held shrinks while the next grows, in 0.35 s ([`carry`](../src/page/people/bodies.ts#carry)), its place is not laid again and its name is not written again, for an agent changes tool every few seconds. Each figure has its book, its hammer and its console from the town's build, at a scale of 0 until it holds them.
- From four agents that wait for the user on one place ([`HEAP`](../src/page/people/heaps.ts#HEAP)), their bubbles are one large bubble over the middle of their places, which says how many wait: twelve bubbles stood in a heap, each over the faces of the row behind. Under four, each one has its own over its head. The bubbles fly there and back in 0.45 s, and the large one grows and shrinks where it rests ([`gathers`](../src/page/people/crowd-heaps.ts#gathers), [`flown`](../src/page/people/heaps.ts#flown)); for a place the camera does not show, with reduced motion, and at the town's opening, they are in place at once. A place is told when its crowd is laid, and nothing is worked out for a place whose bubbles rest ([`flies`](../src/page/people/crowd-heaps.ts#flies)). Each place has one large bubble for it, put with the town: a rounder ball than the small ones', without their mark ([`round`](../src/page/people/bubbles.ts#round)). It comes past its size and settles, and goes slowly then fast ([`swollen`](../src/page/people/heaps.ts#swollen)); the small ones hop on their way ([`hop`](../src/page/people/heaps.ts#hop)). The count is written in the middle of the ball, on the marks' canvas ([`badges`](../src/page/marks/mark-heaps.ts#badges)): as high as the ball's radius on the screen, it follows the bubble as it bobs and grows with it. When the count changes, the ball beats once ([`beat`](../src/page/people/heaps.ts#beat)) and the digits roll, the former ones up and the new ones from under, in 0.3 s ([`TICK`](../src/page/people/heaps.ts#TICK)). The count shows once the place's people read, as the place's far mark, which counts them from afar, goes; one of the two shows, never both. A count whose bubble a building hides goes too, as its subject's sign says the count already: the ray to the camera is cast again only once the bubble or the camera's axis has moved ([`hiddenOf`](../src/page/marks/mark-heaps.ts#hiddenOf)). A small bubble's mark is drawn for the camera: tilted to its pitch, in the middle of the ball as the screen shows it, with its tail to the lower left ([`bang`](../src/page/people/bubbles.ts#bang)). The bubbles turn with the camera's heading, on the processor, only on a frame where the heading changed ([`faces`](../src/page/people/crowd-heaps.ts#faces), [`facing`](../src/page/people/heaps.ts#facing)): a part's movement is added after its instance's matrix, so a program cannot turn it. Who waits is who the place shows waiting: one whose session closes is counted until it has gone.
- While the mouse rests 0.35 s on an inhabitant, a card says what its session is doing; a click pins it as its notebook, with the three last things that happened to the session and a button that opens it ([`notebook`](../src/page/people/notebook.ts#notebook)). Every line is the session's as the page holds it, as Sonar says it to an extension (`PageApi`'s `session`), drawn by Sonar: its state and since when, what it asks or last said, its pull requests, what happened to it ([`nowOf`](../src/page/people/note-card.ts#nowOf), [`moreOf`](../src/page/people/note-card.ts#moreOf)). The server says nothing more for it. A session of a computer linked to this PC has no notebook (`PageSession`'s `remote`). Who is under the pointer is read from where the town draws each figure, from its feet to its head, the nearest to the middle of a body first: in a crowd the bodies cover one another ([`under`](../src/page/people/note-under.ts#under)). The town then shows a hand, read again at each frame. The notebook's frame runs after each of the town's, and does nothing while no pointer is on the town and no card shows. The card never jumps: it comes out of its inhabitant, glides from one to the next, from where it shows, while what it said fades over what it says, and unfolds line by line when pinned; with reduced motion it is in place at once. It stands over the head, bubble included, and comes down with a bubble that leaves for its group's large one, as the inhabitant's name does ([`nameOver`](../src/page/people/heaps.ts#nameOver)), or under the feet when it fits better there, whole on the screen. When the session of a pinned notebook changes, as often as an agent takes another tool, its lines are written again and its cross and its button stay the nodes they are: a press under way and the keyboard's place are kept. A drag turns or slides the town and pins nothing ([`listens`](../src/page/people/note-pointer.ts#listens)); Escape, the cross or a click elsewhere closes the notebook, and so does its inhabitant when it leaves the screen, is seen from afar, or gives its place to another ([`onScreen`](../src/page/people/note-under.ts#onScreen)). A card that does not show, and one that is not pinned, is inert: the keyboard and a screen reader do not find its buttons. The name over the head of the one whose card shows is hidden: the card says it.
- A change during another ends in the last state wanted, and what is under way is never played backwards.
- While the town comes or goes, on another tab, or in a hidden window, the town waits; what changed meanwhile is there at once when it shows again ([`open`](../src/page/sonar/toggle.ts#open)). With reduced motion, every change is there at once ([`controls`](../src/page/render/kit.ts#controls)).
- The subjects' signs, the far marks and the names follow: the town counts its changes, and the labels are written again when the count moves ([`World`](../src/page/render/kit.ts#World), `revision`; [`resaid`](../src/page/signs/sign-again.ts#resaid)).

## A subject that comes

A session opens on a subject the town has no building for: the town builds that subject's block under the user's eyes.

- A town's plan depends on how many subjects it has, so a block cannot be added to an open town. The town holds one instead: the spot the next subject would take in its plan ([`cityGrid`](../src/page/plan/layout.ts#cityGrid), `held`). The spots are ranked whatever their count: a town opened again with that subject as its last one gives it the same block, while the town keeps its side. No town changes for it: the plan is the one of its subjects alone, and a town with no free spot left, or with a quarter's square near that spot, holds none.
- The subject to come is the last of the town's subjects, without a name nor an agent ([`Topic`](../src/page/render/kit.ts#Topic), `held`; [`town`](../src/page/plan/town.ts#town) adds it, the page's flights never see it). Its block is built as any subject's, hidden, with its sign, its scaffold and the spare figures of its square; its building draws from a run of its own, so the others keep their draws. The town's own decor stands on the block meanwhile, as on any free block, in a part of its own with its trees laid in its geometry ([`decorate`](../src/page/plan/city.ts#decorate), [`planted`](../src/page/sites/held-block.ts#planted)). The camera's frame, the town's top and its heights count the buildings that stand, not that one.
- The block has a front of its own, which the parts of a subject's block and that decor read ([`plotted`](../src/page/sites/plot-mask.ts#plotted), `plot` of [`uniforms`](../src/page/render/parts.ts#uniforms)). The ground is never taken away: behind a ragged line that follows the front, the subject's ground lies where the old one lay, cube for cube. Ahead of the front, what stood there is taken down from its top; behind it, what stands on the new ground rises. A subject's ground reaches past its block's edge: it is the held block's when it was put at that block's middle, so a neighbour's ground stays whole. The block never goes ahead of the town's own front: at the opening, and on the way back, it is built and left as the rest.
- The first subject of the page's sessions that the town has no building for takes the block and gives it its name ([`plots`](../src/page/sites/plot.ts#plots)). What stood there leaves as bricks, the cubes a card's building is built of, in 60 % of their spray, each thrown from a point of its surface as it is taken ([`surfaceOf`](../src/page/sites/held-block.ts#surfaceOf)) to land on the building when its height is laid; the building rises bottom-up as they land. A scaffold is born on the ground, goes up with the storey being laid, and goes ([`Rising`](../src/page/sites/scaffolds.ts#Rising)): the scaffold of a pull request, without its sign's bars. Built, the subject is one of the town's: its sign shows, its building and its lamps count in the town's heights, and its sessions take their figures, born from its door ([`lifeOf`](../src/page/plan/town.ts#lifeOf)).
- Merges the subject already has are laid at once before its building rises: no truck comes to a block not built ([`sites`](../src/page/sites/sites.ts#sites)).
- By night the held block looks as any block until its subject comes. The lamps of what stands there light its ground until the subject's own have risen, which light it from then on: the lamps' map is drawn again when the light changes hands ([`lights`](../src/page/render/lamplight.ts#lights), [`parts`](../src/page/render/parts.ts#parts), `hold`). The line of the land's edge is never hidden by the block's front ([`swept`](../src/page/sites/plot-mask.ts#swept), `plot: "kept"`).
- A session the page has not filed yet takes no block ([`wantedOf`](../src/page/sonar/sources.ts#wantedOf), `unfiled`): every new session starts so, and is most often filed a moment later under a subject that exists. The block is for the subject it gets; until then its figure shows only where the town already has a building for the unfiled sessions.
- The held building's look (its square, its flat roof, its mansard's tone, its facade) is drawn while its subject has no name ([`looksOf`](../src/page/buildings/looks.ts#looksOf)), since its parts are counted when the town is built. A town opened again draws it from the subject's name: the same subject, on the same block, may then have another square, roof or facade.
- Nothing is made up: the block is built for a subject one of the page's sessions has. With reduced motion, or for what changed while the town was not looked at, it is built at once, and its sessions' figures are there at once too. One block a town's opening: a second new subject shows at the next one, as does a subject that is not the last one of the page's order, since the town opened again is planned again from its subjects.

## A subject's pull requests

The town shows the work of merging a subject on its building, from the pull requests its sessions follow (`MapPull` of `@sonar/extension`, read as a [`Pull`](../src/page/sonar/pulls.ts#Pull)); the town knows nothing of where they come from.

- A subject's pull requests are the open ones of its sessions, one per address, the failing reading kept when two sessions follow the same one ([`seenOf`](../src/page/sonar/pulls.ts#seenOf), [`joined`](../src/page/sonar/pulls.ts#joined)). The town opening reads them in the sessions of the map's cards, and the open town in the sessions the map tells it: the same reading, so a town that followed shows what a town opened anew shows ([`readMap`](../src/page/sonar/sources.ts#readMap), [`wantedOf`](../src/page/sonar/sources.ts#wantedOf)). What their checks say, and how many of them ended, is the Map's reading (`MapPull`); their tone is the map's, copied in the town ([`toneOf`](../src/page/sonar/pulls.ts#toneOf)). The styles of what the open town shows, its signs, their gauge, its names and its compass, load with the rest of the page too (`styles/world.css`): the start keeps those of the town's icon and its place (`styles/world-icon.css`).
- While a subject has an open pull request, scaffolding stands round the storey to come of its building ([`scaffolds`](../src/page/sites/scaffolds.ts#scaffolds), [`scaffold`](../src/page/sites/scaffold-model.ts#scaffold)): it is born in layers from the building's door, as an inhabitant, and goes back to it when no pull request is open any more. Its planks are red, after a short shake, while a check of one of them fails. One part holds every subject's scaffolding, an instance each, put at its first pull request: a town without a pull request draws nothing more. A part that is not of the crowd shows from its first instance on ([`Part`](../src/page/render/parts.ts#Part), `put`), and is born in layers at every level of the zoom ([`How`](../src/page/render/parts.ts#How), `layers`; [`playLayers`](../src/page/people/layer-play.ts#playLayers)).
- The subject's sign carries a bar for each pull request, in the tone the map gives its mark, filled as its checks end; past five bars, a count ([`signOf`](../src/page/signs/sign-nodes.ts#signOf)). Its name for a screen reader says each one.
- With reduced motion, on another tab, or for a place the camera does not show, the scaffolding is there or gone at once ([`Stage`](../src/page/people/live-steps.ts#Stage), `moves`).

## A subject's merges

A subject's building takes its height from the work merged, not from who works there now.

- The server says a subject's merges on each of its sessions, `topicMerges` (see Sonar's [`docs/architecture/fleet.md`](https://github.com/Krosnoz/sonar/blob/main/docs/architecture/fleet.md)). The town opening reads them in the sessions of the map's cards; a subject keeps the most its sessions say, as a linked computer's session says what its own Sonar counted, and an older Sonar's none ([`readMap`](../src/page/sonar/sources.ts#readMap), [`fleetOf`](../src/page/sonar/sources.ts#fleetOf)).
- Its building has one storey, then one more each time its merges double: after 1, 3, 7, 15… merges, nine storeys at most ([`floorsBy`](../src/page/sites/merge-floors.ts#floorsBy), [`floorsOf`](../src/page/plan/town-blocks.ts#floorsOf)). A storey a merge reached the most in a few days, and every busy subject then stood the same.
- Between two doublings, each merge lays its share of the storey to come ([`raisedBy`](../src/page/sites/merge-floors.ts#raisedBy)): that storey stands under the roof from its first merge, an instance of a part of its own (`rising` of [`partsOf`](../src/page/plan/town-blocks.ts#partsOf)) whose state says the share laid, from its foot. What is laid shows as built; the rest is the storey's bare volume under a building site's net, a green cloth with its mesh, whatever the user's accent ([`netOf`](../src/page/sites/site-net.ts#netOf)). The net gets smaller merge after merge, and while the roof goes up over a storey nothing is laid of yet, it goes up under it ([`lay`](../src/page/sites/rising.ts#lay)). Why a net: [decision 0013](0013-storey-being-laid-under-a-net.md). The roofs are not merged with the still parts ([`mergeStill`](../src/page/render/still-parts.ts#mergeStill)): a storey laid lifts them. Measured, a frame at rest costs the same (3.9 ms either way, 100 agents, 21/11).
- In a city (200 agents and more), every subject's building keeps seven storeys, above its neighbours, as before: its merges do not show there, and no storey is to come.
- The open town keeps each subject's merges as its sessions say them now ([`wantedOf`](../src/page/sonar/sources.ts#wantedOf), [`living`](../src/page/people/live.ts#living)); a subject without a session left keeps those it had.
- A merge told under the open town is laid on its building ([`sites`](../src/page/sites/sites.ts#sites)). A tower crane is born by the building, a length of its mast after the other ([`cranes`](../src/page/sites/crane.ts#cranes)); a truck comes by the block's street with a pallet in the wall's colour, and leaves by the street's other end ([`wheelsOf`](../src/page/sites/delivery.ts#wheelsOf)); the crane takes the pallet up, and the pallet pours bricks, as a card's when the town opens but in 60 % of their spray ([`pourOf`](../src/page/sites/pouring.ts#pourOf), `spray` of [`Grain`](../src/page/arrival/dust.ts#Grain), [`thrown`](../src/page/people/stage.ts#thrown)). The storey shows the merge's share as the bricks land, and the roof, the sign and what reads the town's heights go up with a storey begun ([`lay`](../src/page/sites/rising.ts#lay)). One truck at a time: the next merges wait, three at most, the older ones laid at once ([`works`](../src/page/sites/site-jobs.ts#works), [`begin`](../src/page/sites/site-jobs.ts#begin)).
- What a merge lays is written on the building the town built ([`House`](../src/page/sites/rising.ts#House)): a storey laid whole keeps its instance, so a town that followed its merges shows what a town opened after them shows. Outside a city a building's storeys are not among the traits that tell it from the others ([`designsOf`](../src/page/buildings/designs.ts#designsOf)): one more storey gave it another tint at the next opening. A building shows no fewer storeys while the town is open: fewer merges told show at its next opening.
- Nothing is made up: a truck comes for a merge the server counted, never for a pull request closed without one. A merge that lays nothing (a building at its nine storeys, or in a city) brings none. With reduced motion, or for what changed while the town was not looked at, the building shows its merges at once.
- While a merge is laid, scaffolding stands round the storey to come, as for an open pull request, and goes up with it ([`Works`](../src/page/sites/scaffolds.ts#Works)). The parts of the cranes, the trucks and the pallets take their instances at a subject's first merge: a town without a merge draws nothing more. A city makes none of them.
- A pull request the sign showed open, and its session then says merged, keeps its bar on the sign, full and in the map's tone of a merged one, while its storey is laid or waits to be; without a merge counted within 3 s, it goes ([`landing`](../src/page/sites/landing.ts#landing), [`wantedOf`](../src/page/sonar/sources.ts#wantedOf)). With motion reduced, or for what changed while the town was not looked at, no bar is kept.

## The town's sounds

The open town plays a short sound for each true event it shows on the screen, and is silent otherwise: no sound under it ([`ear`](../src/page/sound/town-sound.ts#ear)).

- The events are those the town plays, told by the part that plays them through its stage ([`Stage`](../src/page/people/live-steps.ts#Stage), `hear` and `knock`): bricks landing, of a block that comes ([`plots`](../src/page/sites/plot.ts#plots)) or of a merge's pallet; a merge in its building, a storey or a building done ([`works`](../src/page/sites/site-jobs.ts#works)); a scaffold standing, a pull request's or, without one, a building site's (a block that comes, a merge), and its checks that fail ([`scaffolds`](../src/page/sites/scaffolds.ts#scaffolds)); an inhabitant born for a session that opens, gone for one that closes ([`step`](../src/page/people/live-steps.ts#step)). A change of state, a tool, the large bubble, the notebook, the truck and the crane are not heard; an agent's question is a moment of Sonar's sounds already.
- Nothing is heard of what the town lays at once: at its opening, for what changed while it was not looked at, for a place the camera does not show, and with motion reduced, which the stage checks too ([`stageOf`](../src/page/people/stage.ts#stageOf)). A town that was frozen (its tab hidden, another view shown) plays at its first frame what it missed: the ear hears nothing of a frame that comes more than a quarter of a second after the last ([`FROZEN`](../src/page/sound/town-sound.ts#FROZEN)).
- Many events of a kind at once are one sound, one a slot: forty sessions that open together are a few bubbles. A sound whose file comes late plays whole, a little later, or not at all ([`LATE`](../src/page/sound/town-sound.ts#LATE)), and none plays once its town is closed. A frame that plays nothing costs the ear nothing.
- The sounds are copies of three of Sonar's files, `pluck`, `wood-tap` and `bubble` ([`assets/sounds/`](../assets/sounds/)), read faster or slower to sound C's five notes, lower than a moment's ([`SOUNDS`](../src/page/sound/town-sound.ts#SOUNDS)). Each goes left or right where its event shows, and from the whole town at a third of its loudness ([`NEAR`](../src/page/sound/town-sound.ts#NEAR), [`FAR`](../src/page/sound/town-sound.ts#FAR)); off the screen it is not heard. Bricks are heard on a beat, a knock a slot at most, one slot in four silent ([`SLOT`](../src/page/sound/town-sound.ts#SLOT)).
- The ear learns where the town's points show after each frame, as the notebook does ([`openWorld`](../src/page/camera/view.ts#openWorld), `listeners`), and forgets it when the town ends.
- It follows Sonar's sounds as `PageApi`'s `sounds` says them: whether a line plays now (the sounds on, the line not cut, this tab the one that plays), their volume, and the page's audio context, woken by the user; a sound that cannot play then is not kept for later ([`townEar`](../src/page/sound/town-ear.ts#townEar)). It decodes its files itself.
- Settings → Sounds shows the town's own section, "The town", as an extension's: after Sonar's groups, where the town is offered only. A line per kind of event, its example and its switch, kept on this device with the moments' switches ([`offerSounds`](../src/page/sound/sound-lines.ts#offerSounds), [`TOWN_LINES`](../src/page/sound/sound-lines.ts#TOWN_LINES)). A line is on until the user cuts it. Sonar knows nothing of them: the town declares them as the machine is known to be offered the town, and plays the examples, its ear loaded at the first one.

## The drawing

The scene is drawn at a density of 2 on every screen ([`rendererFor`](../src/page/render/renderer.ts#rendererFor)), its edges drawn from its depth and normals as an illustration's ink ([`OutlinePassNode`](../src/page/render/outline.ts#OutlinePassNode)), then smoothed ([`pipeline`](../src/page/render/pipeline.ts#pipeline)): the zoom changes what the camera frames, never the size of a pixel on the screen ([`camera`](../src/page/camera/camera.ts#camera)).
A fine detail (a window's frame, a mullion, a shutter, a railing, a furrow, a lamp's post) is left out once it is thinner than 1.5 of these pixels, so that a grid of them makes no moiré from afar ([`detail`](../src/page/render/shapes.ts#detail)); an awning's pale stripes go at 3 pixels, over a canvas of its colour. The shadows leave out the same details, by the sun's texel. A pane or a trim on a wall is lit as its front on all its faces ([`flat`](../src/page/render/shapes.ts#flat)): its sides were a sliver under a pixel that blinked.
On a computer, the drawing goes on under the page's top bar, as the map does; the camera frames what shows below it ([`topOf`](../src/page/camera/size.ts#topOf)).
The points of the page are drawn in a pass apart, on the same grid of pixels, so the edges do not outline them.
The town's own rules (what stands where) are in [`town`](../src/page/plan/town.ts#town), who walks where in [`inhabitants`](../src/page/people/inhabitants.ts#inhabitants); its levels of zoom in [`openWorld`](../src/page/camera/view.ts#openWorld) and [`levelOf`](../src/page/camera/view-frames.ts#levelOf), its signs in [`signs`](../src/page/signs/signs.ts#signs).
A square holds only the agents it shows 16 px tall at the block's level; the rest of a subject's crowd goes to venues of its own near its block, its quarter, and the town grows by as many blocks ([`quartersOf`](../src/page/plan/quarters.ts#quartersOf), [`cityGrid`](../src/page/plan/layout.ts#cityGrid)).
On a subject's square, the furniture stands out of the walkers' round: the benches, lamps and bins of the front between the round and the kerb, the stall and the corner trees at its sides; no inhabitant or robot stands in a part lower than its head ([`furniture`](../src/page/plan/squares.ts#furniture), [`SQUARE`](../src/page/people/grounds.ts#SQUARE)).
A venue is a whole block paved for its crowd; venues stand side by side along their street and join into esplanades, few and dense, so that the town stays built around them ([`quartersBuilt`](../src/page/plan/venues.ts#quartersBuilt), [`VENUE`](../src/page/people/grounds.ts#VENUE)).
A small crowd has a court instead: a paving of its size, of three, at the front of the block's lawn; a subject's square sends there four agents at least, never one alone ([`COURTS`](../src/page/people/grounds.ts#COURTS), [`venueOf`](../src/page/plan/quarters.ts#venueOf)).
A venue is taken only where nothing stands on the blocks on its right, in front of it and in front on its right: from the camera's home, a house there hides part of its paving, so venues grow from the town's front edges inwards ([`spilledOn`](../src/page/plan/layout.ts#spilledOn)).
On a venue, those at work walk several rounds inside one another, spread to its middle when no one stands there: a venue is never a ring round an empty paving ([`walk`](../src/page/people/crowd.ts#walk)).

## Speed of a big town

A town of 10 000 agents keeps its frames short by drawing and writing only what the screen needs, with the same image:

- The inhabitants of each square or venue are one lot of every near part. Each frame, before the town's life, the lots out of the camera's view (with the reach of a shadow) go to the end of the part's arrays, and the part draws only the first ones, in the town's pass and in the sun's ([`lots`](../src/page/render/lots.ts#lots), [`layout`](../src/page/render/lots.ts#layout)). A part's `put` takes the lot as its second argument; `move` reaches the instance wherever it lies ([`parts`](../src/page/render/parts.ts#parts)).
- A walker's matrix is written straight into the part's array, turned about the upright axis only ([`writeTurned`](../src/page/render/instance-matrix.ts#writeTurned)).
- The figures' names are placed at the nearest level only, a class written only when it changes ([`namePlaces`](../src/page/signs/name-places.ts#namePlaces)); they are made hidden and out of the page's layout, and take their place in it when first shown. Their sizes are read once, at the nearest level ([`signSizes`](../src/page/signs/sign-sizes.ts#signSizes)).
- A part's pools of light are split from its geometry once ([`pooled`](../src/page/render/pool-split.ts#pooled)).
